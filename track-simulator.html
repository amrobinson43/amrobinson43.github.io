Track Standing-Start Simulator

Physics-based predictions for the 1 km, 4 km and Hour from a standing start. The model steps the equations of motion forward every 0.01 s. It accounts for a torque–cadence-limited start, out-of-saddle versus seated CdA, velodrome bends, rolling resistance, bearing and drivetrain losses, and system plus wheel inertia. It then finds the fastest way to spend your power.
